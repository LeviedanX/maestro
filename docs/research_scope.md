# Research Scope

## Project

MAESTRO-X v1.0 Quantitative Trading Research Framework


## Research Position

MAESTRO-X v1.0 is evaluated as a quantitative trading research framework.

The objective is not to provide investment advice or guarantee future trading performance.


## Research Boundary

This research does not claim:

- guaranteed profitability
- future market performance
- trading recommendation
- market superiority


## Research Objectives

The study focuses on:

- reproducibility evaluation
- quantitative validation
- robustness analysis
- risk assessment
- research methodology development


## Experimental Principle

The original Pine Script strategy implementation is immutable.

No modification is performed on:

- entry logic
- exit logic
- trading rules
- original TradingView assumptions


## Evaluation Framework

The framework evaluates:

- historical trade outcomes
- performance metrics
- Monte Carlo robustness
- parameter sensitivity
- methodological limitations


## Publication Position

The current objective is academic validation and research documentation.

All conclusions must be supported by experimental evidence.
